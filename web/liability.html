AI responses need review

Model output is generated by third-party AI systems. It can contain errors, omissions, fabricated references, bias or unsafe suggestions. A fluent answer is not proof that it is correct.

Independently verify important information and review code before running it. The app and its outputs are not a substitute for qualified professional advice or independent judgment, especially for decisions that affect health, legal rights, finances or safety.

Availability and pricing can change

Catalog entries and health checks reflect information available at a particular time. A recently responsive model can still fail, time out or reach a rate limit. No model's continued availability, capability or performance is guaranteed.

wfform checks applicable pricing and uses free-route restrictions, but does not control OpenRouter or provider accounts, policies, billing or upstream changes. Free access is subject to their requirements and limits. Review unexpected behavior before retrying a request.

Keep a backup of work that matters

Conversation history and drafts are stored in your browser, not in a wfform cloud account. Browsers can clear or evict data; extensions, interrupted writes, device failures and software errors can also affect it.

Export conversations you want to preserve. Offline shell caching does not guarantee permanent storage and cannot make remote chat work without a network connection. Restoring a deleted conversation is not guaranteed.

wfform is independent of OpenRouter, model providers, GitHub and Google. Their services, websites, terms and data practices are outside this project's control. A link or integration is not a guarantee or endorsement of their output or availability.
