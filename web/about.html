About wfform

A browser workspace for finding free OpenRouter models and keeping your conversations together. Choose a model, understand its capabilities, and make it part of your workflow.

A wrapper with a purpose

wfform stands for Wrapper for Free Open Router Models. It brings model discovery, streaming chat and browser-local history into one workspace. It is an independent project, not an official OpenRouter product.

You choose the model for each conversation. Changing models keeps your unsent text and files in the composer. A conversation that already has messages stays with its original model; a separate workspace carries your unsent input to the new model without mixing histories. An unavailable model never silently turns into a paid alternative.

Know what you are choosing

The catalog refreshes on launch. Search the current models and inspect descriptions, context lengths, pricing, supported inputs and outputs, and request parameters before you select one.

Only compatible models with verified zero prices for the applicable charges can be selected for chat. Recent health checks describe recent observations, not a guarantee of the next response. OpenRouter account requirements, rate limits and provider availability still apply.

Keep your work close

Stream replies, cancel requests, or edit and resend an earlier message. Archive conversations you want to keep, restore them later, or export a backup. History is saved in this browser; it is not automatically synchronized across devices.

Preview Markdown, JSON, YAML and source code. Attachments such as images, audio, video and text files depend on the selected model and provider. The composer checks compatibility and limits before sending. Code previews display text; they do not execute it.

The app has light and dark themes and layouts for narrow and wide windows. Supported browsers can install it as a PWA. Once the offline shell is cached, you can reopen the app and inspect cached model information offline. Remote chat still requires a connection.

Start a conversation

  1. Open wfform and save your OpenRouter API key in Settings. This browser remembers it until you replace or clear it.
  2. Choose a compatible free model from the current catalog.
  3. Write your message, add any supported files, and send.

Free model pricing does not mean unlimited usage. Review the OpenRouter documentation for account setup and service requirements.

Your data and website analytics

Messages and attachments are sent directly from your browser to OpenRouter and the model providers involved in your request. Their terms and data policies apply. Conversation history, drafts and preferences are kept in browser storage; clearing site data can remove them.

This website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits and general usage. Google's tag may use cookies and process browser, device and interaction information. wfform does not add custom analytics events containing prompts, responses, attachments or API keys.

Read the data and analytics explanation and service limitations before using the app. The source repository documents the implementation and accepts public bug reports. Do not include keys or private conversations in an issue.
