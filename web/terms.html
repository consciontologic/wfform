Using wfform

These conditions apply to this website and the wfform application. By using them, you agree to these conditions. If you do not agree, stop using the application.

wfform is an independent browser-based tool for discovering and chatting with free-priced OpenRouter models. It is not affiliated with OpenRouter, Google or the model providers, and does not offer their services on their behalf.

OpenRouter and model providers

You supply your own OpenRouter API key and are responsible for your account, its permissions and its use. You must meet OpenRouter's eligibility requirements and comply with its Terms of Service and the applicable model/provider terms.

The app checks catalog pricing and restricts its chat requests to supported free routes. This does not promise unlimited access or control OpenRouter's account requirements, rate limits, pricing changes or service decisions. Review the information shown before sending and your OpenRouter account settings.

Data, storage and analytics

Chat requests: your browser sends the conversation content needed for a request, relevant attachments and authorization directly to OpenRouter. OpenRouter and participating providers process the request under their own terms and policies, including the OpenRouter Privacy Policy. The wfform project does not operate a chat proxy.

Local history: conversations, drafts, attachments and preferences use browser storage. This is not a cloud backup. Site-data clearing, private browsing, storage limits and changing browser or origin can make history unavailable. Use export to keep your own backup and the app's delete controls to remove stored conversations.

Saved connection: a key explicitly saved in Settings is remembered in this browser until you replace it, clear it or remove site data. It is used for direct OpenRouter requests and is accessible to this browser's user. It is not included in the offline application cache.

Website analytics: every website page includes Google Analytics using measurement ID G-P3K2ZN7YTL. The Google tag measures visits and usage and may use cookies and process browser, device, page and interaction information. wfform does not add custom analytics events containing prompts, replies, attachments or API keys. Google's own collection behavior and your browser settings also affect what is processed.

See Google's Privacy Policy and its Analytics privacy information. Browser privacy controls, content blockers and Google's available opt-out tools may limit analytics. Keep personal information and credentials out of page URLs and public issue reports.

Changes and support

The app, model catalog and these conditions may change. This page shows its latest revision date. Review material changes before continuing to use the app. There is no promised uptime, response time or support schedule.

Report reproducible problems through the public source repository's issue tracker. It is a public development channel; do not post API keys or private content there. Account and provider issues may need to be handled with OpenRouter directly.
